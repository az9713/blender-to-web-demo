# Concept to Geometry Note

The generated concept image is used for silhouette, palette, strap readability, and a quick visual target: three separate metallic bands around a cyan luminous core.

The executable geometry is governed by `config/sculpture-parameters.json` and the equations in `docs/blender-to-web-project-handoff.html`. Where the image is ambiguous or visually impossible, the math wins. The outer, middle, and inner bands are built as independent swept superellipse straps with conservative spacing, closed transport-frame seams, and browser-side runtime pivots.
